# CCNA

This folder collects CCNA3 v7 lab materials. The assignments and configurations cover VLANs, EtherChannel, inter-VLAN routing, OSPF, access control lists, NAT, and related switching topics. The Packet Tracer files are lab assets; open them with compatible Packet Tracer software.

## What I did

I worked through CCNA3 switching and routing labs, configuring and documenting network devices and reviewing the corresponding topologies. The work includes VLANs, EtherChannel, OSPF, ACLs, NAT, DHCP snooping, and Dynamic ARP Inspection, with Packet Tracer files for hands-on practice.
